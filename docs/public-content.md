# Public content guide

The public site is intentionally compact. Payload owns the copy, media, and ordering; the web app owns the page patterns and form behavior.

## Main routes

- `/` — homepage, featured stories, locations, and enquiry form
- `/shipping` — service overview
- `/shipping/ocean-freight` and `/shipping/logistics-solutions` — focused service context
- `/locations` — illustrative location records and filters
- `/news` — The Dispatch newsroom and its topic/country filters
- `/company` — CMS-managed company pages

The public header links to the service overview, locations, newsroom, company section, language selector, and enquiry form. Links without a language prefix redirect to the visitor’s saved language; `/en` is the default.

## Editing safely

Use **Homepage** in Payload for hero and footer copy. Use **Pages** for structured public pages: rich text, image, feature, callout, and related-page blocks are the approved patterns. Published edits revalidate the affected public content automatically.

All demo stories, locations, and service claims are illustrative. Do not use them as evidence of live service availability, rates, offices, or shipment status.
