# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Demo reviewers and developers evaluating a simplified public-facing shipping and logistics website.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate a compact public website with a small set of pages, customer-facing forms, and a newsroom. It exists as a private demo rather than a public product.

## Positioning

A deliberately simplified demo that takes its functional and visual reference from Maersk's public website while retaining only a few essential pages, forms, and newsroom content.

## Operating Context

The public site reads managed newsroom and homepage content from Payload. Visitors can browse news, submit a contact message, and subscribe to editorial updates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a Next.js web application backed by Payload.
- Scope is intentionally small: a few pages, a few forms, and a newsroom.
- It is not intended for public release.
- Demo imagery may use Unsplash assets.

## Brand Commitments

- Primary brand color: `#bd1874`.
- Light theme only.
- Maersk's public website is the requested design reference, simplified for this demo.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing homepage, news index and article pages, contact form, newsletter form, and Payload content models.
- No production claims, customer testimonials, or proprietary media have been supplied; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make the core shipping and newsroom experience easy to assess in a short demo.
- Keep the page and interaction scope intentionally focused.
- Prioritize readable content and clear forms.
- Use imagery only as demonstrative editorial or atmospheric support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Optimize for readability and preserve accessible form and navigation behavior.
